Welcome to RingdateR (web)

A browser port of the RingdateR crossdating tool — detrend tree-ring series, crossdate them pairwise or against a chronology, inspect the results, and export data, plots and a run report. Everything runs locally in your browser; no data leaves your machine.

  1. Load & options — load undated series (and optionally a dated chronology), or use the example data.
  2. Choose a detrending method and lead/lag limits.
  3. Pick Pairwise or Chronology mode and Run analysis.
  4. Filter the results table, view plots, build a chronology, then download or generate a report.

Build chronology

Grow a chronology iteratively — crossdate one candidate series at a time against the current mean chronology, review the alignment plots, then approve the suggested lag (or your own override). Uses the current detrend settings and, if a chronology was loaded, seeds from its members.
