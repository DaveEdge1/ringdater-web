      Welcome to RingdateR (web)

      Crossdate tree-ring series in your browser — detrend, compare series against each other or a dated chronology, and build chronologies interactively. Everything runs locally; no data leaves your machine.

      What would you like to do?

      Explore

      Crossdate undated series against each other (pairwise) or against a dated chronology. Inspect the results table and interactive plots.

      Build

      Build a new chronology from scratch — pick an anchor series, then crossdate and approve candidates one at a time (or auto-build).

      Extend

      Extend an existing dated chronology — crossdate new undated series against its mean and grow it.

      Learn

      New to RingdateR? Take a guided, hands-on tour using the bundled example data.

      Formats: .csv, .txt, .rwl, .pos, .lps, .xml (TRiDaS/Tellervo), Ring-Measurer. First column = years/increment.
